END-TO-END PIPELINE

01 · INGEST

SAR scene

Sentinel-1 GRD via NASA Earthdata (ASF DAAC). Georeferenced, time-stamped.

→
02 · DETECT

Ship candidates

YOLO trained on SSDD, HRSID and OPEN-SSDD, or classic CFAR. Tiled for large scenes.

→
03 · ESTIMATE

Wake and heading

Brightest ray from the ship gives the wake bearing; heading is the opposite direction.

→
04 · FUSE

AIS association

Kalman-predict each AIS track to the SAR time, gate by distance and time, assign with the Hungarian method.

→
05 · SCORE

Anomaly alerts

Dark vessels, course mismatch, stale AIS and stopped-but-wake cases are scored and queued for human review.

MISSION BRIEF

Oceans are too large to patrol, optical satellites are blind through cloud and night, and ships can switch off AIS. Synthetic Aperture Radar sees through weather. This system detects ships in SAR images, estimates their direction of travel from wakes, cross-checks them with AIS broadcasts and flags suspicious vessels for a human operator.

Console data on the tactical map is a demonstration scenario with made-up vessels.
